The **software projects of Quentin Wood** include video games developed for FBLA competitions and tools intended for community use.[^projects]

## FBLA video games

Wood developed three notable game projects for [[wikipedia:Future Business Leaders of America|FBLA]] competitions:

* *[[Cluck Run]]*
* *[[Collector of Souls]]*
* *[[Graduation Day]]*, which won first place at the 2026 FBLA National Computer Game and Simulation Development event

## Wildlife Survey Platform

The [[Wildlife Survey Platform]] is intended to support wildlife surveys. Work is underway toward integrating it with trails and public parks in Jefferson County, Colorado.

## Wildfire Mapping Project

The [[Wildfire Mapping Project]] was developed to help Wood's local community during the annual wildfire season by presenting wildfire information through a mapping interface.
