I’m **Quentin Wood**, a computer science student at Colorado School of Mines and a Junior Software Developer at Touch of Care. I grew up in rural Colorado, and I’m interested in building software that is useful beyond the screen.

## Growing up in Colorado

I was born in Montrose and grew up in Olathe. I attended Maslow Academy and Olathe Elementary School, then continued through middle and high school in Olathe. School, work, and being outdoors were all part of that experience.[^story]

My early jobs included yard work and baling hay. During my junior year of high school, I began working part-time at Touch of Care. Moving from those first jobs into software development helped connect programming with real operational needs.

## Learning by building

Programming, robotics, and electronic circuits are interests I pursue outside class as well as in my studies. My independent projects have been a way to learn by making something concrete.

FBLA gave that work a focus: I built *[[Cluck Run]]*, *[[Collector of Souls]]*, and *[[Graduation Day]]*. In 2026, *Graduation Day* won first place nationally in Computer Game and Simulation Development. My community projects include [[Wildfire Mapping Project|wildfire mapping]] and a [[Wildlife Survey Platform|wildlife survey platform]].

## Where I’m headed

At Mines, I’m pursuing computer science with an expected graduation date of May 2030. I’m interested in software engineering, full-stack development, AI, automation, and healthcare technology.[^brief] Those interests connect the practical software work I do today with the problems I want to explore next.

## Life outside programming

I ran varsity cross-country and track throughout high school and still enjoy distance running. I qualified for state cross-country in all four years and state track in three. I also completed a marathon to raise funds for my FBLA chapter.

Outside running, I enjoy hiking in the mountains, exploring nature, fishkeeping, plants, and terrariums. Curiosity about how things work carries across those interests.
