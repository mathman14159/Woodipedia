***Collector of Souls*** is a video game developed by [[Quentin Wood]] as one of his three notable FBLA software projects.[^collector]

## Development context

The game was created for FBLA competition. It is part of a group of projects that includes *[[Cluck Run]]* and *[[Graduation Day]]*, the latter of which won first place nationally in 2026.
