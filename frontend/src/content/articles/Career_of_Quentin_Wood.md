The **career of Quentin Wood** began with manual and seasonal work during high school and later developed into a part-time position in software development.[^career]

## Early work

Wood held several jobs while attending high school. His early work included yard work and baling hay.

## Touch of Care

During his junior year of high school, Wood accepted a part-time position at Touch of Care in Montrose, Colorado. He continues to work for the organization as a junior software developer while attending the [[wikipedia:Colorado School of Mines|Colorado School of Mines]].

## Independent software work

Outside his employment, Wood regularly works on independent and competition-based software. These projects include FBLA video games, a wildlife survey platform, and a wildfire mapping project. See [[Software projects of Quentin Wood]].
