The **athletic career of Quentin Wood** includes four years of varsity cross-country and track, one season of basketball, and continued participation in distance running.[^athletics]

## Cross-country

Wood competed on his high school's varsity cross-country team throughout all four years of high school. He qualified for the state meet in each season and contributed to two regional team championships.

At the regional meet, Wood placed eleventh individually during his junior year and fifth during his senior year. He credits athletics with helping him form close friendships and lasting memories.

## Track and field

Wood ran varsity track for four years. He qualified for the state meet in his sophomore, junior, and senior seasons after missing qualification as a freshman. He holds his high school's records in the indoor two-mile and the 4×800-meter relay.

## Basketball

As a sophomore, Wood joined the basketball team without knowing how to play the sport. Through consistent work, dedication, and a positive attitude, he progressed enough to earn an appearance in a varsity game by the end of the season.

## Distance running

Wood completed a full marathon as a fundraiser for his FBLA chapter. He continues to participate in distance races and running workouts.
