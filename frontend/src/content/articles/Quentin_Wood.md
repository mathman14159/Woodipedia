**Quentin Wood** (born August 8, 2007) is a computer science student and junior software developer from Colorado. He attends the [[wikipedia:Colorado School of Mines|Colorado School of Mines]], where he is pursuing a bachelor's degree in [[wikipedia:Computer science|computer science]]. Wood is known for his software projects, participation in the [[wikipedia:Future Business Leaders of America|Future Business Leaders of America]] (FBLA), and high school distance-running career. In 2026, his video game *[[Graduation Day]]* won first place in the FBLA National Computer Game and Simulation Development event.[^bio]

## Early life

Wood was born in Montrose, Colorado, on August 8, 2007. He attended Maslow Academy during his first years of school. After moving to Olathe, Colorado, he transferred to Olathe Elementary School and continued his education in Olathe through middle and high school.

## Education

Wood graduated from high school as valedictorian and *summa cum laude* and served as head boy. He received honors connected with the National Honor Society (NHS), THS, Student Council, FBLA, and other school organizations. He also received several scholarships, including recognition as an MEM Scholar, to help fund his education at the Colorado School of Mines.

At Mines, Wood is pursuing a bachelor's degree in computer science. A more detailed account appears in [[Education of Quentin Wood]].

## Career

During high school, Wood performed a range of jobs, including yard work and baling hay. In his junior year, he began working part-time for Touch of Care in Montrose. He continues to work there as a junior software developer. See [[Career of Quentin Wood]].

## Software development

Wood spends much of his free time developing software. Three of his best-known high school projects—*[[Cluck Run]]*, *[[Collector of Souls]]*, and *[[Graduation Day]]*—were created for FBLA competitive events. *Graduation Day* earned first place nationally in 2026.

His other work includes a [[Wildlife Survey Platform]] intended for integration with trails and public parks in Jefferson County, Colorado, and a [[Wildfire Mapping Project]] designed to help his local community during the annual wildfire season.

## Awards and achievements

Wood won regional FBLA titles in Financial Math and Computer Game and Simulation Programming. He later won both the state and national titles in Computer Game and Simulation Programming. His high school honors included valedictorian, *summa cum laude*, and head boy. A complete summary appears in [[Personal achievements of Quentin Wood]].

In athletics, Wood was part of two regional championship cross-country teams. He placed eleventh individually at the regional meet during his junior year and fifth during his senior year. He also holds his high school's indoor two-mile and 4×800-meter relay records.

## Athletics

Wood competed in varsity cross-country and track throughout all four years of high school. He qualified for the state cross-country meet in each of those years and for the state track meet in three, missing qualification only as a freshman.

As a sophomore, he joined the basketball team without prior playing experience. His work ethic, dedication, and positive attitude helped him earn an appearance in a varsity game by the end of the season. Wood also completed a full marathon as a fundraiser for his FBLA chapter and continues to participate in distance races and running workouts. See [[Athletic career of Quentin Wood]].

## Personal life

Wood's interests include robotics, electronic circuits, and programming. He keeps plants and several terrariums and also enjoys fishkeeping. His outdoor interests include hut hiking, spending time in the mountains, and exploring nature.
