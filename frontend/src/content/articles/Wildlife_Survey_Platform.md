The **Wildlife Survey Platform** is a software project developed by [[Quentin Wood]] to support wildlife surveys.[^wildlife]

## Project overview

The platform is intended to help organize wildlife-survey work associated with trails and public parks. Wood is working toward its integration with public lands in Jefferson County, Colorado, commonly known as Jeffco.

## The problem

Wildlife-survey work needs a way to collect and organize observations associated with trails and public parks. The platform supports that work through a React/TypeScript application.[^stack]
