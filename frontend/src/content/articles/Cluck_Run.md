***Cluck Run*** is a video game developed by [[Quentin Wood]] as one of his three notable FBLA software projects.[^cluck]

## Development context

The game was created for FBLA competition. It preceded Wood's nationally recognized game *[[Graduation Day]]* and is part of a group of projects that also includes *[[Collector of Souls]]*.
