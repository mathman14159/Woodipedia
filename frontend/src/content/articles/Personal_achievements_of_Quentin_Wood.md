The **personal achievements of Quentin Wood** span academics, software-development competitions, and high school athletics.[^achievements]

## Academic honors

Wood graduated from high school as valedictorian and *summa cum laude* and served as head boy. He received honors through the National Honor Society, THS, Student Council, FBLA, and other clubs. His scholarships included recognition as an MEM Scholar, which helps fund his studies at the [[wikipedia:Colorado School of Mines|Colorado School of Mines]].

## FBLA

Wood earned two regional FBLA titles: one in Financial Math and one in Computer Game and Simulation Programming. He also earned state and national titles in Computer Game and Simulation Programming. His game *[[Graduation Day]]* placed first at the 2026 FBLA National Computer Game and Simulation Development event.

## Cross-country and track

Wood was a member of two regional championship cross-country teams. He placed eleventh individually at the regional meet in his junior year and fifth in his senior year. He qualified for the state cross-country meet in each of his four high school seasons and for the state track meet in three seasons.

Wood also holds his high school's records in the indoor two-mile and the 4×800-meter relay.

## Endurance running

Wood completed a full marathon as a fundraiser for his FBLA chapter. After high school, he continued taking part in distance races and running workouts.
