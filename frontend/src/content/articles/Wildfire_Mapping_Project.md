The **Wildfire Mapping Project** is a software project developed by [[Quentin Wood]] to help his local community during Colorado's annual wildfire season.[^wildfire]

## Project overview

The project uses a mapping interface to make wildfire information useful to people in Wood's local area. It reflects his interest in applying software to community and environmental needs.

## The problem

Colorado’s wildfire season makes local fire information relevant to community preparedness. This project organizes and visualizes wildfire information through a mapping interface, with evacuation and response as the motivating context.[^brief]
