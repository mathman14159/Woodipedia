The **education of Quentin Wood** began in Montrose, Colorado, and continued in Olathe before he enrolled at the [[wikipedia:Colorado School of Mines|Colorado School of Mines]].[^education]

## Early education

Wood attended Maslow Academy in Montrose during the first years of his education. After his family moved to Olathe, he transferred to Olathe Elementary School. He remained in Olathe for middle and high school.

## High school

Wood graduated as valedictorian and *summa cum laude* and served as head boy. He received honors through the National Honor Society, THS, Student Council, FBLA, and other clubs.

During high school, he combined academics with varsity athletics, student organizations, software development, and part-time employment. He received multiple scholarships, including recognition as an MEM Scholar.

## Colorado School of Mines

Wood attends the Colorado School of Mines and is pursuing a bachelor's degree in [[wikipedia:Computer science|computer science]]. His interests in the field include software development, robotics, electronic circuits, geographic mapping, and machine learning.

## Student projects

Wood's high school software work included three FBLA game projects: *[[Cluck Run]]*, *[[Collector of Souls]]*, and *[[Graduation Day]]*. The last of these won the 2026 FBLA National Computer Game and Simulation Development event. His other work is listed in [[Software projects of Quentin Wood]].
